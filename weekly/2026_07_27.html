Hello! Here's something to explore this week in the studio:

Last week we paid attention to how our feet provide the grounding needed to create opposition. This week let's think about what is actually creating the movement.

When our feet are on the footbar – or in the straps – it's easy to think that our feet are doing the work. But our feet aren't the goal. They're simply our connection to the apparatus – the next layer in what we began exploring with our feet last week.

Instead of thinking about pushing with your feet, try thinking about straightening your legs. Rather than making circles with your feet, imagine your entire leg moving from the hip. The feet stay connected, but the movement comes from something much bigger.

Sometimes, changing where we place our attention changes the quality of the entire exercise. The body begins to organize itself differently because we're thinking about the movement instead of the point of contact.

This week, notice where your attention goes during exercises like Footwork, Stomach Massage, Frog, or Leg Circles. Are you focused on your feet... or on the movement of your legs? You may discover that when your feet simply maintain the connection, your legs become freer, stronger, and more coordinated.

See if changing what you're thinking about changes how the exercise feels.
