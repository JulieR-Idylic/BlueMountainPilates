Good morning 😊

In Pilates, “advanced” doesn’t always mean learning new exercises. Often, it means returning to the foundational work with a more practiced eye – finding greater depth, connection, control, and precision within movements that may already feel familiar.

The exercises themselves may not change, but your understanding of them does. This mindset in how we approach an exercise – and feel it in our bodies – is exactly what inspired our upcoming workshop.
