This week's Pilates thought – Why do we repeat the same exercises?

Have you ever wondered why we do so many of the same exercises each time you come to Pilates?

In his 1945 book, Return to Life Through Contrology, Joseph Pilates emphasized the importance of repetition in developing control, coordination, and mastery of movement.

Joseph Pilates understood the importance of repetition. By practicing the same exercises regularly, we develop greater control, coordination, and mastery. And by returning to familiar exercises, we have an opportunity to recognize just how far we've progressed. An exercise that once seemed impossible might now feel manageable. Maybe your balance has improved, your movements are more controlled, or you've discovered strength you didn't have before.

So the next time you find yourself doing an exercise you know by heart, pay attention to how it feels compared to when you first learned it. Sometimes the best way to see how far you've come is to revisit where you started.

Pilates is a practice, not perfection! 😊
