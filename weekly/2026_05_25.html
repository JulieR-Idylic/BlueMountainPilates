Sometimes the most challenging part of Pilates isn’t the exercise itself — it’s learning how to create length, opposition, and connection within the movement.

Those ideas can feel subtle at first, but they’re often what transforms a familiar exercise into something entirely different in the body. In a group class setting, we don’t always have the time to fully explore the details and the “why” behind those concepts.

That deeper way of approaching the work – and feeling it in the body – is exactly what inspired our upcoming workshop.
