Here’s a little bit of Pilates “wisdom” to start your day 😊

It's easy to think an exercise begins when the carriage starts moving. In Pilates, the work actually begins before that. It starts with how you organize your body, find your alignment, connect to your center, and prepare your breath. That moment of preparation sets the tone for everything that follows.

This week, before you begin each exercise, pause for just a moment. Feel your starting position. Notice where your weight is. Take a breath. Then begin the movement with intention.

You may be surprised how much that single moment changes the quality of the entire exercise.
