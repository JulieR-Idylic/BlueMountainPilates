Hello! One of the beautiful things about Pilates is that the basics never stop teaching us. 😊

We've spent the last few weeks noticing our eyes, our feet, and where movement begins. This week, let's notice another small detail that has a surprisingly big influence on the entire exercise—your tailbone.

We don't often think about it, but the direction your tailbone is reaching helps organize your spine and pelvis. A small change in that intention can completely change the quality of the movement.

Think about how that changes from one exercise to another. In Elephant or Knee Stretches – Round Back, reaching the tailbone toward the spring bar helps maintain the rounded shape of the spine. In Footwork, allowing the tailbone to stay long creates a stable foundation as the legs straighten. In Short Box, the direction of the tailbone helps support the shape you're creating – whether you're rounding the spine for Round Back or sitting tall in Flat Back.

This week, don't think of your tailbone as simply following along. Instead, ask yourself: "Where is my tailbone reaching?"

You may discover that a small change in that intention makes the entire exercise feel more organized, connected, and controlled.
