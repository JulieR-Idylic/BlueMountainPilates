Good morning! Here’s a little bit of Pilates “wisdom” to start your day 😊.

In Pilates, “advanced” doesn’t simply mean doing more or harder exercises. The exercises may look the same, but the work becomes deeper, more connected, and more challenging in a different way.

Your focus shifts to how deeply you can connect to each movement — bringing more awareness, control, precision, breath, flow, and concentration into the work. These are the six principles that guide the system of Pilates.

You may notice your instructor using more detailed or specific cues to help guide that connection — small adjustments in breath, alignment, or control, and often the idea of working in opposition (reaching in two directions at once).

This week, try choosing just one familiar exercise — maybe Footwork, Elephant, or Circles — and explore where you can find that sense of opposition within the movement.
