This week's Pilates thought 😊

Last week, we explored the idea that where your eyes go, the body often follows. I invited you to notice where your gaze was directed during a few familiar exercises. Did your gaze change the way the exercise felt?

This week, let's take it one step further: when do your eyes move? In Pilates, the eyes shouldn't create the movement – they should support it. Rather than leading with the head or chin, allow your gaze to follow the shape of the exercise.

Let's revisit those exercises with an "advanced eye":

  • Down Stretch: Let the lift of your chest guide your eyes from the floor toward the ceiling.
  • Elephant: Looking through your legs helps support the rounded shape of your spine.
  • Short Box Flat Back: Allow your gaze to travel with your torso rather than staying fixed on the mirror.
  • Straps Pulling: Let the lift of your chest carry your eyes instead of lifting your chin.

See if one small change in your gaze changes the entire exercise. I look forward to hearing what you notice!
