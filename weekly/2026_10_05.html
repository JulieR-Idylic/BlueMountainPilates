This week's Pilates thought 😊

One of the things that makes our group classes a little different from a traditional group fitness class is that not everyone is necessarily doing the same thing at the same time.

Pilates is an individual practice, even when we're practicing together. Your instructor may give you a different exercise, spring setting, modification or progression than the person next to you. You may be working independently on something familiar while your instructor introduces something new to someone else – and then comes back to help you find another layer in your own movement.

That's also why the person next to you isn't necessarily the person to follow! 😊 It's tempting to glance over when you're not sure what comes next or how to set something up, but their exercise – or even their version of the exercise – may not be the one your instructor wants for you. When you're unsure, ask your instructor. That's what we're there for.

This connects nicely with what we've been talking about the past couple of weeks with progression and independence. Independence doesn't mean figuring everything out on your own or copying someone else. It's developing ownership of the work you know while still leaning on your instructor for direction when you need it.

We're practicing together, but each person is developing their own Pilates practice. 😊
