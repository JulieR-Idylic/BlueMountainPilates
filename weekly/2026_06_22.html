Good morning and Happy Father’s Day to all the dads in our Community! Here’s a little bit of Pilates “wisdom” to start your day 😊

You’ve all heard me mention the "last inch" of a movement and how important it is. Whether it's bringing the carriage all the way home, finding the final length in Elephant, or maintaining your plank in Long Stretch, that last little bit often requires the most control.

It's tempting to focus on the big movement – pushing the carriage away or moving it quickly through space. But in Pilates, the quality of the movement is often revealed in how we finish it.

This week, pay special attention to the last inch of Long Stretch (Plank), Elephant, and the Knee Stretch Series.

  • In Long Stretch, can you keep the same plank shape all the way until the carriage returns gently home?
  • In Elephant, can you bring the carriage completely in without losing the lift of the abdominals?
  • In Knee Stretches, can you arrive at the stopper quietly, without letting the springs take over?

You may discover that's where the work has been hiding all along.
