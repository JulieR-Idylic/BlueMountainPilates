This week's Pilates pearl 😊

You've probably heard me say "Momentum is the Pilates killer!" But does that mean we should always move slowly? Not necessarily!

I came across an interesting article from Pilates Physics last week called Reformer Momentum: Slow vs. Fast, and of course, being the data geek that I am, I literally drooled over the science and graphs! 😆

One of the things I found particularly interesting is how speed changes the resistance we experience during an exercise. Think about Footwork on the Reformer. When we move more quickly, momentum can carry the carriage through part of the movement, meaning our muscles aren't necessarily experiencing the same load (resistance) at full extension as they would if we moved more slowly.

But that doesn't mean faster is wrong! Different speeds can serve different purposes, depending on what we're trying to accomplish. I especially encourage you to read the section titled "Is momentum bad in Pilates?" It offers a great opportunity to think about how and why we change the tempo of an exercise.

So this week, pay attention to your speed during Footwork. What changes when you slow down? What happens when you speed up? And most importantly, are you controlling the movement, or is momentum doing some of the work for you?

Here's the full article. I hope you enjoy the graphs as much as I did! 😊
