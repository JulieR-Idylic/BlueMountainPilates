Good morning 😊

We've spent the last several weeks developing our "advanced eye," one small detail at a time. Each week we've added another layer - not necessarily changing the exercises we're doing, but changing how we experience and understand them.

Before I introduce the next layer, let’s take a few minutes to revisit the concepts we've explored using the links below:

  1. Don’t forget the importance of that last inch!
  2. Get set before you move: organizing your body
  3. Where are your eyes looking and How your eye movement changes the exercise
  4. The importance of your feet to stay grounded
  5. Where do we place our attention in an exercise?
  6. Organizing your spine with your tailbone

Now let's add another layer to our "advanced eye" – the feedback we get from the apparatus.
Whether you're working on the Reformer, Cadillac, or Wunda Chair, the apparatus is constantly giving you information about how you're moving.

This week, let’s use our senses and pay attention to what the apparatus is telling us. What do you hear? What do you feel? What do you see?

Don't worry yet about what it means – just notice. We'll start exploring some of those messages next week.
