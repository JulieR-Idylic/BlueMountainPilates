Pilates is a practice — and like any practice, it takes time. It’s normal for things to feel unfamiliar, a little awkward, or even like you don’t “feel” much at all. The understanding doesn’t happen all at once — it builds gradually as your body learns how to organize and support the movement. Stay with it. The understanding builds gradually.
