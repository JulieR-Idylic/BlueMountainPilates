Good morning, Pilates Community!

Imagine if every Pilates class came with a label: Beginner Reformer, Core Blast, Booty & Legs, Athletic Pilates, Cardio Jumpboard.

Now imagine arriving after a poor night's sleep, with a sore shoulder, a tight hip, or simply a different energy level than you had last week. Would that label really tell us what your body needs that day?

One of the things I value most about the semi-private format we have at the studio is that it allows the work to adapt to the person. While everyone may be performing the same exercise, the focus, challenge, and goal can be different for each individual. One client may be learning the movement for the first time, while another is exploring greater depth, control, and connection within it.

That's why you'll occasionally see us offer special experiences such as Jumpboard classes and workshops. These give us an opportunity to explore a specific aspect of the Pilates system together.

These special offerings add variety and exploration, while our semi-private classes continue to provide the individualized instruction that allows each person to progress at their own pace.
