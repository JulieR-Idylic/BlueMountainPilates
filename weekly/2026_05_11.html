Happy Mother’s Day to all the Moms in our studio community!

I’m excited to share a very special announcement. TEN Pilates Studio will be hosting a 2-hour workshop focused on deepening your Pilates work by diving deeper into the concepts that support the exercises. The workshop will be led by local master Pilates teacher and founder of our studio, Patti Jo Amerein.

For those of you who know Patti Jo, you know her passion for Pilates and the way she shares that passion through clear, detailed instruction. I am incredibly honored to continue learning from her as both a mentor and an inspiration in my own practice.

In this fun and informative workshop, you will learn how to increase the effectiveness of your practice by applying foundational concepts such as Opposition, Reach, Connection, Stabilization, and Mobilization, and how these elements help elevate the intensity of every exercise within the Pilates system.

This workshop is designed for anyone wanting to advance their work by refining familiar exercises, exploring the “why” behind movement, and discovering new levels of control and precision.

Expect thoughtful, focused work in a small group setting.
