This week's Pilates thought 😊

You may have heard me say in class that momentum is the Pilates killer! But why is that?

When momentum takes over, part of the movement is happening for us. We may still get from point A to point B, but we've given up some of the control, precision and muscular work that Pilates asks of us.

And this isn't just about the Reformer. Momentum can sneak into exercises throughout the entire Pilates system – on the Mat, Cadillac, Chair, or Ped-o-Pul.

Try slowing down just enough to notice: Are you controlling every part of the movement, or is momentum doing some of the work for you? Taking momentum out doesn't necessarily mean moving slowly. It means you are in control of the movement from beginning to end.
