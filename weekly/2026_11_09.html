This week's Pilates wisdom and a request – Stop Counting and Start Feeling!

You know those repetition counts I put on your exercise cheat sheets? Well, this week I want you to IGNORE THEM! 😆

Yes, I put them there for a reason. They give you a general guide to help you move through your repertoire in our 60-minute sessions. But with the exception of The Hundred (our stamina builder and cardio booster!), the number isn't nearly as important as what you're getting out of the movement.

Have you asked your instructor, “how many?” when doing an exercise and received the response “it isn’t that important - if you lose count, just do two more and move on!”? Well – here's your challenge for this week. Instead of counting repetitions, give each one something different to think about.

  • First rep: Where is your body? Check your alignment and notice how you're positioned.
  • Second rep: What are you feeling? Which muscles are working? Are you feeling the exercise where you expect to?
  • Third rep: Are you breathing!? Are you breathing with the movement, or have you been holding your breath?! 🤣

Don't stop exploring there! Maybe your body needs a few more repetitions of a particular exercise today. Or maybe you've gotten what you needed and you're ready to move on. I often really feel that second rep and in my mind – I am wanting to savor that engagement/stretch!

The goal isn't to finish a certain number of repetitions. It's to make each one count!
