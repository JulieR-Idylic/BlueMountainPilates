Good morning 😊 Here’s your pearl of Pilates wisdom this week:

So...what did you notice? 😊

Last week, I asked you to use your senses and pay attention to the feedback you're getting from the apparatus. This week, let's focus on one of those senses: what do you hear?

The sounds of the apparatus can tell us quite a bit about the quality of our movement.

Think about Footwork. What do you hear as the carriage comes home? Does it arrive quietly at the stopper, or do you hear it bump? That sound may be telling you that you've stopped resisting the springs before the movement is actually finished. Remember our earlier focus on that last inch—the exercise continues all the way home.

Now listen to the springs themselves. (And no, I don't mean the couple of particularly "groany" springs we have in the studio right now! 😊) Knee Stretches are a great place to experiment. Listen instead to the rhythm and consistency of the springs as the carriage moves out and in. Does that sound stay smooth and even? What happens as you change your speed or rhythm? The springs can give you clues about whether your movement is controlled or whether momentum has started to take over.

The goal isn't to make the apparatus silent—or to analyze every squeak and groan! It's to begin recognizing when the sounds created by your movement give you useful information.

This week, tune in to the sounds of your apparatus and see what they tell you about the way you're moving.
