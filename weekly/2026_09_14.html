This week I want to touch on Why is Teaser so hard?!

Teaser is one of those Pilates exercises that can make even a very strong person wonder what on earth is happening. 😊 That's because Teaser isn't simply a test of abdominal strength.

Think about what we're asking the body to do. We're balancing on a relatively small base while asking the center to manage the weight of both the upper body and the legs. At the same time, we're organizing the spine, supporting the legs and trying to arrive at the top with control.

And when Teaser gets difficult, our natural instinct is often to try harder – muscle our way up, throw the arms forward, or use momentum to get there. But remember what we've talked about the past couple of weeks: momentum and precision. Getting to the final position isn't really the point. How you get there is where the Pilates happens.

That's why making Teaser better isn't necessarily about getting stronger or trying harder. Sometimes it's about doing less – taking out the momentum, finding more precision, and noticing where you lose control.

So if Teaser is challenging for you, don't think only, “I need stronger abs.” Instead, get curious about which part of the movement is asking you to compensate or muscle your way through it.

After all, Pilates is a practice – not perfection. 😊
