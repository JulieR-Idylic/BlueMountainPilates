This week's Pilates thought:

If we can do so much on the Reformer, why do we have all that other equipment in the studio?

One of the things I love about classical Pilates is that it's a complete system, not just a bunch of exercises on the Reformer. The Cadillac, Chair, Barrels, and Mat all have a role to play in helping us develop our Pilates practice.

Sometimes we're working on an exercise and just can't quite find the right connection or figure out how to organize our body to make it happen. That's when trying something on another piece of apparatus, with a slightly different technique, can make all the difference!

For example, if you're struggling to find your C-curve during Short Box Round Back on the Reformer, we might try a Half Roll Down on the Mat or work with the Roll Down Bar on the Cadillac. Or if Teaser is giving you trouble, we might try it with the Push Through Bar. Sometimes that little change is all it takes for something to click! And the real benefit comes when you can take that connection back to the Reformer.

Of course, we can't always explore every piece of equipment during a regular session. Between the time we have and what's available, we have to make choices about where to focus. But the goal isn't to do more exercises or use more equipment. It's to help you find better connections and organization in your body so you can get more out of the exercises you're already doing.

That's one of the things I love about having the full Pilates system in our studio. There are so many ways to help something finally click! 😊
